Adaptation for BREACHLINE:
- Converted the self-contained glTF to GLB with Blender 4.5.2 LTS.
- Removed simultaneously attached weapon options from the character.
- Preserved 17 named skeletal animations, sampled at the source rate of 30 fps.
- Normalized rest-pose height to 1.8 m and feet to origin; +Y up and front +Z.
- Corrected penetrating walk/run frames with a vertical Root animation lift (maximum 0.111 m); airborne frames retained. Other clips key the unchanged Root position to clear offsets when switching.
- Kept the original materials and bone names. No textures or other dependencies required.
- AK and Shotgun were exported separately under weapons/ with original bone-local transforms.
